# SIGNALCHECK
### Don't just verify information. Verify its journey.

An AI-powered platform for investigating digital media, detecting potential manipulation and tracing information back to its sources.

## What is SIGNALCHECK?

From suspicious screenshots and deepfake videos to cloned voices and misleading livestreams, SIGNALCHECK aims to bring media analysis and source investigation together in one place.

## Two Ways to Investigate

**1. Upload & Investigate**

Upload images, screenshots, videos, audio recordings, documents or URLs. SIGNALCHECK analyzes the content, investigates its sources and identifies potential inconsistencies.

**2. Live Detection**

Analyze webcam and microphone feeds or supported external livestreams for potential media manipulation and suspicious claims.

## What We're Building

- **Image Forensics** — Examine potential image manipulation and discover matching sources.
- **Deepfake Detection** — Investigate potentially AI-generated and manipulated media.
- **Video Analysis** — Examine frames, edits and reused footage.
- **Audio Forensics** — Investigate synthetic voices and audio alterations.
- **Source Investigation** — Find original publications and compare different versions.
- **Evidence Journey Graph** — Connect discovered sources, media and investigation findings.

## What Makes It Different?

SIGNALCHECK doesn't simply label information as real or fake. It investigates where it came from, how it may have changed and what evidence supports the findings.

## Initial MVP

- Image and screenshot analysis.
- AI-powered claim extraction.
- Browser-based source investigation.
- Content comparison and evidence collection.
- Evidence Journey Graph and investigation reports.
- A limited live webcam and microphone analysis workflow.

Advanced video, deepfake, voice-cloning and external livestream analysis are additional development goals.
